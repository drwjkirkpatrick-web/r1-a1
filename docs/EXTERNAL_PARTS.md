# R1-A1 External Build Resources

Curated links to community astromech designs, blueprints, 3D-printable
files, and parts suppliers. R1-A1 does not host or redistribute these
files — this page points you to the original creators and communities.

> **Note:** Some vendors require a free [astromech.net](https://astromech.net/)
> forum membership to purchase parts. Join the club first — it unlocks
> parts runs, blueprints, and decades of build knowledge.

## 7. Recommended Build Path for R1-A1

1. **Join astromech.net** (free) — unlocks parts runs, blueprints, and forum knowledge.
2. **Download the Dave Everett styrene plans** from the astromech.net downloads section for reference dimensions.
3. **Download the Mr Baddeley v2 file set** from his Patreon (free tier) for the 3D-printed structural parts.
4. **Read the Printed Droid FAQ + Print Guide** for filament choices and print settings.
5. **Decide on dome:** printed (Baddeley files) or aluminum (Granite Earth hydro-formed or astromech.net spun dome parts run).
6. **Decide on frame:** printed (Baddeley frame) or aluminum (JAG DXF cut locally / Frank's pre-cut).
7. **Source electronics** — reference the Printed Droid wiring diagrams for motor drivers, slip-rings, and dome rotation.
8. **Follow a build journal** (I Like To Make Stuff or Kevin Rye) alongside R1-A1's own `docs/BUILD.md`.
